Reference Description:

ABOUT THIS PET

Bramble will drop whatver he is doing for a treat. He loves to play fetch, he'll play for as long as you want, bringing the ball back every time. He is curious and always on the move, checking every corner and trotting back now and then to check in with his person. Once he gets to know someone, he leans into them with a big smile while they pet him.

HOUSEHOLD FIT

Bramble came to Hollowbrook Animal Rescue with his brother. Apart from his brother, he has not been tested with other dogs, or with cats. He is more comfortable with adults, and would do best in an adult-only home without children.

TRAINING

Bramble plays fetch, and treats get his attention fast. On walks he pulls and changes direction often as he takes in everything around him. He willing steps into his harness or a slip lead for walks.

GOOD TO KNOW

Bramble takes a little time to warm up to being touched: he growls when he is not ready yet, most of all when a stranger reaches for his legs or belly.

Through the Hollowbrook Animal Rescue senior program, Bramble's adoption fee is waived and he is eligible for up to $1,500 in medical reimbursement.

Hollowbrook Animal Rescue will review Bramble's full history with you before you meet him.
