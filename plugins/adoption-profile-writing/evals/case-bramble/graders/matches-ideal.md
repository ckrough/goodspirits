Reference Description:

ABOUT THIS PET

Bramble is always on the move, exploring and looking for something interesting to sniff, happy to do his own thing, and every now and then he comes back to check in with you before he is off again. Offer a treat and you have his full attention. He also likes to play fetch.

HOUSEHOLD FIT

Apart from his brother, he has not been observed with other dogs, or with cats. With people, he likes to set the pace: quick movements spook him, and he needs time to warm up before he is touched. On a walk he has barked at strangers, then leaned against them while they pet him.

TRAINING

Bramble has been patient with volunteers fitting his harness, and he has let people pick him up and hold him for a while.

GOOD TO KNOW

Bramble is uncomfortable being touched until he has had time to warm up, most of all around his legs and belly.

Through the Hollowbrook Animal Rescue senior program, Bramble's adoption fee is waived and he is eligible for up to $1,500 in medical reimbursement.

Hollowbrook Animal Rescue will review Bramble's full history with you before you meet him.
