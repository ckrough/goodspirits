Reference Description:

ABOUT THIS PET

Bramble has places to be. He is always on the move, exploring and looking for something interesting, happy to do his own thing, and every now and then he comes back to check in with you before he is off again. Bring out a ball and the exploring can wait: he will play fetch. Offer a treat and you have his full attention.

HOUSEHOLD FIT

Bramble gets riled up when he and his brother are together, and the two have done better since living apart; on his own he is the calmer one. Apart from his brother, he has not been tested with other dogs, or with cats. With people he likes to set the pace: quick movements spook him, and he needs time to warm up before he is touched. On a walk he has barked at strangers, then leaned against them while they petted him.

TRAINING

Bramble has been patient with volunteers fitting his harness, and he has let one pick him up and hold him for a while.

GOOD TO KNOW

Bramble is uncomfortable being touched until he has had time to warm up, most of all around his legs and belly, which can make putting on his harness slow going. He growls as a warning.

Through the Hollowbrook Animal Rescue senior program, Bramble's adoption fee is waived and he is eligible for up to $1,500 in medical reimbursement.

Hollowbrook Animal Rescue will review Bramble's full history with you before you meet him.
