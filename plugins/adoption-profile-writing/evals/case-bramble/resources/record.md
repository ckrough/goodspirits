## Day 18, behavior/trainer notes (internal)

Sweet when he warms up. LOVES treats and will play fetch! Calmer than his brother but gets riled up when he's with his brother. It's clear Bramble was handled in a way that took away his choice and autonomy in the past. He will get snappy and may try to bite if he's feeling pressured. Spooked by quick movements. Go slow and let him set the pace! Keeping the harness on will help him not feel too much pressure.

## Day 24, volunteer walk note

Very anxious on the walk, constantly changing directions and whining a lot, but very cooperative with handling and harnessing and even let me pick him up and hold him for a while. Met some strangers and barked at them but leaned on them with mouth open happily while they pet him.
Checklist: strong on leash 3, leash reactivity 2, shy/fearful 1, jumpy/mouthy 2.

## Day 35, volunteer walk note

His harness is supposed to be left on, but it doesn't fit him well so it keeps falling off. I very slowly harnessed him. We left and I noticed that one of his legs was out, so we went back in and I re-harnessed him and clipped a second leash to his martingale collar as a back up. While we walked his harness slipped off most of the way. I was able to gently remove it and we finished the walk on the martingale collar and leash.
Another volunteer helped me try to refit a smaller harness for him. He was really patient, but eventually did snap at the air, so we ended there. We noticed that his touch sensitivity seemed to be greatest on his legs.

## Day 48, memo (pop-up)

Bramble has a behavior profile, as well as handouts (decompression, cooperative care, shy and fearful dogs, fear-free handling) in his file that must be gone over and given to ANY adopter or foster.

## Day 49, volunteer walk note

Put his harness on very slowly. Double leashes, with a leash clipped to his martingale collar. Harness came off while walking. He was panting the whole time we were walking.

## Day 51, matchmaking/ideal home notes

## Day 51, matchmaking/ideal home notes

## Day 55, behavioral team notes (updated)

Bite history! Uncomfortable being touched until he has time to warm up. Sensitive around his legs and belly, which can make harnessing harder, so feel free to just use the slip lead until he's more willing to be harnessed. WATCH BODY LANGUAGE AND VOCALIZATIONS. He will growl as a warning.

Sweet and curious. Content doing his own thing and checking in every now and then. Bramble tends to be ON the whole time, always looking for something and moving around. Taking him to a quiet area to get some relaxing time in is always beneficial.
