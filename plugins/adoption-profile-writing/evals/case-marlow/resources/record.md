Marlow warms up a bit quicker to new people than others. However, he has mild sensitivity to new people that we've seen a few times. At least 2 no-contact visits (parallel walks down the driveway) before going into a play yard.

## Month 44, Diamond Dog adoption support

Level 3: pre- and post-adoption support from the contracted behaviorist (severe resource guarding of food and other things).

## Month 44, behavior/trainer notes (internal)

Marlow was lying on his raised bed with soft, loose body language. When I opened his kennel he approached with soft eyes, mouth open, tail up and wagging gently. He leashed easily and walked great with me, responded very well to his name, and is super food motivated! In a yard he explored and ran around for a minute, then when I called him he ran up to me and sat for treats. He responds to "sit" very well, and his "touch" cue is great.
Recommendations: continue basic cues, especially proofing "touch" and "drop it"/"leave it"; impulse-control exercises (stay, wait, leave it); continue playgroups.
