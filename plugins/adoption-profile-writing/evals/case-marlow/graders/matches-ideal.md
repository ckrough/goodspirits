Reference Description:

ABOUT THIS PET

Marlow is a hiker. Take him out on the trails and he is good for miles, and after a day out he has stretches out in the grass. Come to a stream and he may wade right in. Some days he is cuddly instead and just wants to sit in the shade, close to you. He is easy company in town too: he has walked through the pet store and left the toys on the shelves alone.

HOUSEHOLD FIT

Put Marlow in a group of dogs and he digs, wrestles, or just wanders alongside his favorites. When he met a cat he gave it the space it asked for and barked when it hissed. With people he does not know, he likes room and he would rather they not lean over him.

TRAINING

Call Marlow's name and he comes running and sits for a treat. He knows touch too. He walks easily on leash, pulling a little only when he is excited.

GOOD TO KNOW

Marlow guards food, treats, bones, and his space: he has barked to keep a bag of treats he had grabbed.

Marlow is eligible for the Hollowbrook Animal Rescue senior program: Hollowbrook Animal Rescue waives his adoption fee and reimburses up to $1,500 in qualifying medical expenses.

Marlow is a Hollowbrook Animal Rescue Diamond Dog: Hollowbrook Animal Rescue provides training and guidance to help you settle him into his new home, at no cost to you.

To meet Marlow, please submit an adoption application. Hollowbrook Animal Rescue will then begin the matchmaking process, go over his full history with you, and set up a meet-and-greet. Thank you for considering Marlow!

Required facts, in any wording:

1. When he met a cat he gave it space and barked when it hissed.
2. With people he does not know, he wants room: he would rather they not lean over him.
3. He plays in groups of dogs.
4. He guards food, treats, or things he has claimed, and has barked to keep one, described as a behavior rather than with the term "resource guarding".
5. He loves hikes.

Every other fact in the reference is optional: leaving it out does not count against the profile, and neither do facts the reference does not mention. How quickly he warms up to new people is optional too, so a profile that says he warms up fairly quickly does not fail for it. Wording, order, and length may differ.
