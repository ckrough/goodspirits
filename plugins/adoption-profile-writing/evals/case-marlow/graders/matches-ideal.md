Reference Description:

ABOUT THIS PET

Marlow is a hiker. He has done three miles at a stretch and waded into a stream along the way, and when the hike is over he likes to roll in the grass. On a hot day he would rather sit in the shade, close to you. He has gone along on trips into town and to the pet store, leaving the toys on the shelves alone.

HOUSEHOLD FIT

Put Marlow in a group of dogs and he digs, wrestles, or just wanders alongside his favorites; he has played with many. When he met a cat he gave it the space it asked for and barked when it hissed, and the person who introduced them would not leave him alone with one. With people he does not know, he likes room: he would rather they not lean over him.

TRAINING

Call Marlow's name and he comes running and sits for a treat. He knows touch too. He walks easily on leash, pulling a little only when he is excited.

GOOD TO KNOW

Marlow guards food, treats, chews, and his bed: he grumbles or barks when someone reaches for something he has claimed.

Marlow is eligible for the Hollowbrook Animal Rescue senior program: Hollowbrook Animal Rescue waives his adoption fee and reimburses up to $1,500 in qualifying medical expenses.

Marlow is a Hollowbrook Animal Rescue Diamond Dog: Hollowbrook Animal Rescue provides training and guidance to help you settle him into his new home, at no cost to you.

To meet Marlow, please submit an adoption application. Hollowbrook Animal Rescue will then begin the matchmaking process, go over his full history with you, and set up a meet-and-greet. Thank you for considering Marlow!

Required facts, in any wording:

1. He would do best in an adult-only home without cats.
2. He has played well with many dogs in groups at the shelter.
3. He guards food, treats, and things he has claimed, and grumbles or barks when someone reaches for them, described as a behavior rather than with the term "resource guarding".
4. He prefers that strangers not lean over him or get in his face.
5. He loves hikes and trail walks.

Every other fact in the reference is optional: leaving it out does not count against the profile, and neither do facts the reference does not mention. How quickly he warms up to new people is optional too, so a profile that says he warms up fairly quickly does not fail for it. Wording, order, and length may differ.

PASS when the profile states every required fact and contradicts none of them. FAIL when a required fact is missing or contradicted, and name which.
