Grade the profile in profile.md. If the file is missing or empty, FAIL. Grade only the text under the four headings.

Rubric dimensions 6 and 10, identity-forward opening and narrative flow.

Opening: the first sentence under ABOUT THIS PET opens on who the animal is or a concrete behavior ("On a couch at the shelter, she stretches out beside whoever is reading"), not on breed, age, size, medical status, or a generic adjective ("Meet this sweet senior").

Flow: label ABOUT THIS PET, HOUSEHOLD FIT, and TRAINING each by what most of it does. "lived": it shows what spending time with the animal is like, through shared moments, contact, play, or routines. "reported": connected and accurate, but an evaluator's log of commands known and reactions. "list": standalone attributes with nothing linking one observation to the next. Storytelling must not stretch the facts: no invented inner states ("he dreams of"), no detail no one could have observed, no filler that carries no fact.

PASS when the opening is on the animal, at least two of the three sections are lived and none is a list, and nothing is embellished. FAIL otherwise, naming the opening's problem, each section's label, or the embellishment.
