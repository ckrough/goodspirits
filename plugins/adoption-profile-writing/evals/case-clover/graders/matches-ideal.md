Reference Description:

ABOUT THIS PET

On her walks, Clover goes nose down and follows the trail, tracking scents and steering you toward the next good smell. In a fenced yard she explores on her own, trotting back every few minutes to check in for a pet and a treat. She leans into scratches behind the ears and gives kisses. Clover came to the shelter when her family situation changed and they could no longer care for her.

HOUSEHOLD FIT

Clover has played in groups with other dogs. In her foster home, she sniffed noses with the family cat and has stayed away since. She has lived with children, and she has done well meeting kids from preschoolers to teenagers. Clover goes along easily on outings: she rides quietly in the back seat, greets visitors, and has dozed off on a dog bed beside live music.

TRAINING

Clover is housetrained and crate trained. In her foster home she walks into her crate, lies down, and sleeps the afternoon away until the kids get home from school, and she has spent an evening home alone with no accidents and nothing chewed. Once a walk gets going, she is easy on the leash.

GOOD TO KNOW

Clover whines and pulls with excitement as the leash goes on, and usually settles once she is out the door. When a dog walks past the fence, she sometimes barks and runs along it, tail wagging.

Hollowbrook Animal Rescue will review Clover's full history with you before you meet her.
