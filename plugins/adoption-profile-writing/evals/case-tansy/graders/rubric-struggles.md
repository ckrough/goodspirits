Grade the profile in profile.md. If the file is missing or empty, FAIL. Grade only the text under the four headings.

Rubric dimension 11, struggle balance, with the struggle framing from dimension 10. Count the distinct struggles the profile states anywhere: difficulties, dislikes, fears, guarding, reactivity, things being worked on, each behavior once. Placement limits ("would do best in a home without cats"), unknowns ("has not been tested with cats"), and the support and closing lines (the Diamond Dog message, a senior program, the history-review line) are not struggles.

PASS when all of these hold:
- The profile names one or two struggles, no more and none fewer.
- Each is a behavior a reader can picture, framed with where it happens, what helps, or how it is improving, without threat or clinical words ("aggressive", "resource guarding", "reactive") and without softening it until no behavior is left.
- The struggle sentences are a small share of the narrative, roughly a fifth or less, not counting the support and closing lines.
- The profile gives the adopter no handling instruction or management plan ("let him come to you", "never reach for his bowl", "needs a home that will work on his guarding").

FAIL when any one fails, naming the struggles counted and which condition failed.
