Grade the profile in profile.md. If the file is missing or empty, FAIL. Grade only the text under the four headings, and skip the program blocks in GOOD TO KNOW (a senior program, the Diamond Dog message, the history-review line).

Rubric dimension 2, behavioral concreteness: claims are behaviors a reader could picture or check ("knows sit", "pulls toward squirrels on leash"), not personality labels. Positive labels count as labels as well as negative ones: friendly, gentle, quiet, sweet, loving, affectionate, loyal, smart, obedient, well-behaved, mellow, laid-back, easygoing, energetic, active, high-energy; shy, timid, fearful, nervous, anxious, sensitive, dominant, submissive, hyperactive, excitable, reactive, aggressive, stubborn, pushy. A label heading the behavior that shows it is fine ("He is gentle: he takes treats softly from a hand"); a label standing in for a behavior is not. Naming a trait to deny it ("not aggressive", "no resource guarding", "does not guard") is a label too.

PASS when the claims are mostly behavior and at most one label stands in for a behavior. FAIL when two or more labels stand in for behaviors, when a trait is named to deny it, or when a struggle is given only as a trait word ("he is reactive"). Name each one found.
