Grade the profile in profile.md. If the file is missing or empty, FAIL. Grade only the text under the four headings.

Rubric dimension 3, observed, not promised: every claim reports what was seen, rather than predicting what the animal will do in a new home ("great with all dogs", "will love your kids", "will settle in fast").

These are observations, not promises:
- A typical moment told in the present tense, built from what the animal does again and again, with or without the reader in it ("Throw a tennis ball and he is off", "On her walks she goes nose down and follows the trail", "Call his name and he comes running").
- Behavior that would rule a household out, described as it was seen ("when he sees a child, even across a parking lot, he tucks his tail and tries to get away").
- An unknown stated as unknown ("has not been tested with cats", "how she does with children is not known"), with no hedge or guess attached.

Whether the card states a limit on the home ("would do best in an adult-only home") is graded per case; do not count it either way here.

PASS when the profile is observed throughout, with at most one soft generalization. FAIL when it makes a promise or an absolute claim about the new home or the animal's future there, guesses at an unknown ("probably fine with cats"), or contradicts its own disclosures (calls a trait unknown in one place and asserts it in another). Name each one found.
