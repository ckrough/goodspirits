Grade the profile in profile.md. If the file is missing or empty, FAIL. Grade only the text under the four headings, and skip the program blocks in GOOD TO KNOW (a senior program, the Diamond Dog message, the history-review line).

Rubric dimension 1, factual register: every sentence carries a fact a reader could check.

PASS when the profile has at most one emotional flourish or platitude. Warm, plain phrasing attached to an observed behavior ("climbs into your lap the first chance he gets") and playful phrasing that rests on one ("a fetch champ") are not defects. FAIL when the profile has two or more of: superlatives ("the best dog ever"), cuteness framing ("adorable"), emotional appeals or pleas with no behavior behind them ("he just needs someone to love him"), or platitudes ("forever home", "so much love to give", "heart of gold", "steal your heart", "one of a kind", "deserves a second chance", "won't last long"). Name each one found.
